## Honest caveats / what is still owed

1. **The 0.71 is a post-hoc refinement, not the a-priori verdict.** It came from a drawdown-motivated vol-target fix (swapping a per-asset vol proxy for the portfolio's own trailing realized vol, which captures correlation). Legitimate and Sharpe-improving as a side effect, but the kill-rule verdict rests on 0.61.
2. **Borrow cost on short legs is NOT yet modeled.** Engine A shorts down-trending ETFs; only 2 bps of turnover cost is charged. Charter requires "realistic costs." Add borrow (cheap for liquid ETFs, unlikely to flip 0.71) + a cost-sensitivity check. Tracked as an open task.
3. **Drawdown -35% sits at the top edge** of the 25-35% mandate.
4. **Full-sample == OOS here only because Engine A is parameter-free.** The moment any parameter is tuned, a formal purged/embargoed walk-forward becomes mandatory (harness supports it).
