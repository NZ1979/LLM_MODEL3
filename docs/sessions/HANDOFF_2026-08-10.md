### 2. Found a much larger cost that had been invisible

Modelling borrow surfaced **margin financing on leverage**. Engine A runs at
**3.70x mean gross, 6.81x peak**, borrowing 1.30x NAV in cash on 66% of days.
Root cause: `LEV_CAP = 2.5` caps the vol-target *multiplier*, not gross exposure -
pre-overlay gross averages 7.37x.

Financing is roughly ten times the borrow cost. A flat-rate sweep straddled the
bar (0.48 at 2%, 0.37 at 4%, break-even 3.4%), so it could not adjudicate
anything, and choosing a friendlier flat rate after seeing 0.37 would have been a
goalpost move.

Operator decisions: financing was **not** folded into the locked kill rule (which
names only slippage, commissions, borrow - tightening a locked bar after seeing
results is the same class of error as loosening it). It became a **separate
pre-paper realism gate**, pre-registered in `docs/FINANCING_SPEC.md` with the rate
spec fixed in advance (FRED `DTB3` + 100 bps debit, `DTB3` - 25 bps credit) and
committed unrun before the measuring run.

**Gate result: PASS at 0.45**, 73% of rolling years positive. `DTB3` averaged
1.77%; financing drag 3.276%/yr.

### 3. The finding that decided the session

The gate passes at `DTB3` +100 bps but **fails at +200 bps** (0.39). Retail Reg T
margin runs 300-600 bps over benchmark, and a Reg T account cannot hold 6.81x
gross overnight anyway. **The account type that could hold the exposure is the one
whose financing costs sink it.** Engine A's edge is real; the engine is not
implementable.

`docs/ENGINE_A_CAPPED_SPEC.md`: Engine A with one change, `GROSS_CAP = 2.0` (the

parameter), `ex_ante` mode, everything else inherited. One cap, one evaluation,

tested against synthetic weights only so its performance was unobserved when the

| condition | result | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1. borrow-complete >= 0.40 | 0.44, 78% | PASS |
| 2. measured financing >= 0.40 | 0.42, 76% | PASS |
| 3. no gross breach of 2.0x | 0 breaches | PASS |
| 4. clears bar at `DTB3` +200 bps | 0.40 (below unrounded) | **FAIL** |
| 5. clears bar with zero cash credit | 0.38 | **FAIL** |

Condition 4 failed on the third decimal. That is still a fail - the point of a
pre-registered bar is that a near-miss is a miss.

Condition 5 was added mid-session, after a synthetic smoke test revealed a capped
book carries a large positive cash balance so financing can become a net *credit*.
Without it, A-2 would have been scored partly on the assumption that a broker pays
`DTB3` - 25 bps on idle cash. Adding it before any run could only make the gate
stricter. The concern was real: removing the credit costs 0.04 of Sharpe.

**Interpretation.** The edge is not purely a leverage artefact - a 47% cut in
exposure cost only 25% of the Sharpe, and the capped engine still beats the 0.31
mechanical baseline. But it is too thin to implement safely: 0.04 of headroom,
dying to variation well inside normal broker terms.

## What is still not modelled (stated plainly)

Slippage beyond a flat 2 bps/side (the a-priori spec's break-even is 0.4 bps once
financing is charged - it is highly sensitive here); borrow *availability* and
recall risk; historical borrow rates are assumptions, not observations; margin
path-dependence (a -48.9% drawdown at 3.70x gross would likely force
deleveraging before the modelled recovery). Full list in
`docs/COST_COMPLETENESS_RESULTS.md` section 6.
